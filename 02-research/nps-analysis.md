# NPS Feedback Findings: Streak Break & Re-engagement

*For Marcus. Source: 10 raw NPS comments from Streakly users.*

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once (Ranked by Frequency)

### 1. Breaking a streak leads directly to disengagement or deletion — 3 mentions
- "I hit a 20-day streak, missed one day, and it reset to zero. I haven't opened the app since. Felt pointless to start over."
- "Deleted after 3 weeks. The moment I lost my streak the whole thing lost its meaning."
- "The streak is the only thing keeping me engaged, but the second I lost it, I was done."

### 2. Users want a recovery/forgiveness mechanism instead of punishment — 3 mentions
- "I want Streakly to feel like a coach that helps me get back on track, not a scorekeeper that punishes me for missing a day."
- "I broke my streak once and there was no way to recover it. Other apps let you freeze a streak. Why not this one?"
- "I wish it would make coming back easier instead of making me feel like I failed and have to start from scratch."

### 3. Notification frequency and tone are off — 2 mentions
- "The first week was genuinely fun. After that the daily reminder just started to feel like nagging."
- "The notifications feel random. I got three in one afternoon and just turned them all off."

**Single-mention data points worth flagging (don't independently rank as themes, but reinforce #1 and #3):**
- "The home screen looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks away. Nothing acknowledges where I am." *(reinforces #2 — no acknowledgment of user context)*
- "Love the lessons. I just forget it exists after a couple of days. If it pulled me back with something useful I'd come back." *(the flip side of #3 — not just too many notifications, but a lack of a useful one)*

## Praise vs. Complaints

Every piece of praise in this batch comes attached to a complaint — there's no purely positive comment in the set.

**Praise (partial):**
- "The first week was genuinely fun." (followed by a complaint about nagging reminders)
- "Love the lessons." (followed by a complaint about forgetting the app exists)

**Complaints:** the remaining 8 comments, and the latter half of the 2 above, are all pain points — dominated by the streak-punishment theme (6 of 10 comments touch it directly or indirectly).

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

1. **Build a streak recovery mechanism (freeze/grace period).** Directly requested by name ("other apps let you freeze a streak"), and the single highest-leverage fix — it addresses the punishment perception and the churn/deletion outcomes at once.
2. **Acknowledge user context on return instead of a flat reset.** The home screen and post-break flow show the same blank state regardless of history. Users explicitly want the app to reflect where they actually are, not force a "start from scratch" feeling.
3. **Fix notification frequency and relevance.** Complaints run in both directions — too many/random notifications get turned off entirely, while one user forgets the app exists without a useful nudge. The issue isn't volume alone, it's that the notifications aren't targeted or useful.

*Note: this feedback corroborates the existing hypothesis and interview synthesis already in progress — the punishing streak reset with no comeback path.*
